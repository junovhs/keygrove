Your course

RQWERTY is a free, guided touch-typing course on a standard QWERTY keyboard. Its 36 lessons cover letters, capitals, punctuation, numbers and longer mixed passages, with four optional code lessons.

Start with F, J and Space. We introduce a few new keys, mix them with familiar movements, then use them in fresh text. Continue takes you to what comes next. You do not need to plan a practice session or understand a scoring system.

The seven chapters are Roots, Home, Canopy, Undergrowth, Bark, Rings and Flow. The Course book shows your place and lets you revisit earlier lessons. The optional Code chapter can be enabled in Settings after Bark.

Use a physical QWERTY keyboard. Begin by typing the highlighted character, or press Enter. Spaces count too: the small dot in a passage is a space. A wrong key stays highlighted until you type the right one. There is no countdown. Escape restarts a passage; Tab moves through the interface controls.
