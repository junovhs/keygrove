# Data banks

- `sentences.json`, `quotes.json`, `code.json` — hand-authored for KeyJam (public domain / pangrams).

Generic words come from one vocabulary, `VOCABULARY` in `src/curriculum/headline.ts`: the authored early words
(`PRACTICE_WORDS` in `src/curriculum/language.ts`), a few sentence-starting staples, then `EVERYDAY_WORDS` from
`src/curriculum/movements.ts`. The Flow grove's Common Words use `COMMON_WORDS`, the first 200 of the same list.
`EVERYDAY_WORDS` is generated by `research/typing-movements/scripts/export_movements.py`. It is SUBTLEX-US
(Brysbaert & New) crossed with Peter Norvig's Google Books word counts, under the research everyday-word filter.
Sources are listed in `research/typing-movements/SOURCES.md`. **Before a commercial release, confirm the licence
terms of both sources**.

The previous `words.json`/`top200.json` (google-10000-english, web-derived) were removed in CURR-51.
